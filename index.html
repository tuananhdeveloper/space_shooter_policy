Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 02, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Tiny Byte Labs ("we", "us", or "our") handle personal information when you download, install, or play our mobile application Space Shooter (the "Game").

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) directly from you (such as your real name, physical address, or phone number). However, the Game may automatically process certain non-personal data necessary for gameplay and operational performance:

  • Device & Gameplay Preferences: Local storage settings (e.g., unlocked ships, upgrade levels, audio preferences, high scores) saved directly on your device via local application preferences.
  • Technical Diagnostics: Anonymized crash logs and device performance metrics provided automatically by operating systems (Google Play Console).

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The Game may integrate third-party SDKs for advertising or analytics. These third-party services may collect non-personal identifier data subject to their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services: Used for game distribution and optional achievements/leaderboards. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • AdMob / Unity Ads (if applicable): Used to deliver non-intrusive in-game advertisements. Device advertising IDs (GAID) may be processed for relevant ad serving and fraud detection.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Our Game is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately so we can promptly delete such information.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your game progress (gems, ship unlocks, preferences) is stored locally on your device. We do not maintain external servers that store your personal gameplay records. While local data cannot be guaranteed against physical device loss or uninstallation, we take reasonable measures to ensure secure local processing.

5. Your Choices & Data Rights

You can clear all locally stored game progress at any time by clearing the application data through your Android device settings or by uninstalling the Game.
